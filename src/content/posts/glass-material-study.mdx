## Render data as the scene controller

This article is written as MDX, but the 3D scene above is selected from frontmatter. The post declares a `pbr-turntable` preset, a material, a camera preset, and motion behavior. The page then mounts a React Three Fiber canvas only when the scene block becomes visible.

</section>

<section id="roughness">

## Material parameter notes

<div class="parameter-card">
  <strong>Roughness</strong><br />
  Lower roughness produces sharper highlights. The timeline switches the scene into a macro camera state for closer inspection.
</div>

<div class="parameter-card">
  <strong>Transmission</strong><br />
  Glass-like materials should keep roughness low and use environment lighting for stronger silhouettes.
</div>

<div class="parameter-card">
  <strong>Fallback shape</strong><br />
  If no GLB asset is provided, the scene renders a procedural icosahedron so the article remains interactive.
</div>

</section>
